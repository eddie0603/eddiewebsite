Welcome to the World of Fun!

Get ready to explore the magic of LEGOLAND Malaysia Resort — where imagination comes alive and every corner brings a new adventure!
From thrilling roller coasters to colorful LEGO worlds, there’s something exciting for everyone to discover!

Water Park

Ride the surf at the LEGO® wave pool, float along the Build-A-Raft lazy river, climb and slide down the most awesome tube and body slides. Frolicking in the sun just gets merrier, with interactive play structures from Joker Soaker to DUPLO® Safari which make each day “splash-tacular”!  

Theme Park

Step into a world built entirely from imagination! Explore over 70 LEGO®-themed rides, shows, and attractions designed for families and thrill-seekers alike. Conquer the Dragon roller coaster, earn your license at Driving School, or join a medieval quest at the LEGO® Kingdoms. Every corner bursts with color, creativity, and adventure — making every visit a “brick-tastic” experience you’ll never forget! 

SEA LIFE Aquarium

Dive beneath the waves and explore a magical underwater world at SEA LIFE Malaysia! Walk through the ocean tunnel surrounded by sharks, rays, and colorful reef fish. Discover fascinating sea creatures up close and learn how we can protect their habitats. With interactive touch pools and educational exhibits, every visit is a “fin-tastic” journey of discovery for both kids and adults!
